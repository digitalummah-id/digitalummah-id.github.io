Full Features & User Guide

Each feature explained, with how to use it. Click the image in each section to see the full-size screenshot.

Lightweight

01Only 1 MB, no installation

The whole app is only about 1 MB. There is no installer and no complicated setup: extract the ZIP and run it. It works well on older computers and from a flash drive.

Both Arabic fonts are stored inside the app data and loaded straight into memory, so you never install a font into Windows and the text looks the same on every computer.

Accuracy

02Text and translation that follow Kemenag

All 114 surahs and 6,236 verses follow the Qur'an Kemenag text, with its punctuation, pause marks, and verse numbers. The translation also comes from the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs.

Under each verse you can show the Latin reading, the translation, and footnotes where available. Each one can be turned on or off.

Language

03Indonesian and English versions

Two packages are available: Al-Qur'an Digital Indonesian and English. Buttons, menus, and translation follow the language of the package you use.

The language option is also in the Settings menu, right at the top.

Navigation

04All 114 surahs in one panel

The SHOW SURAH LIST button opens the full list of 114 surahs with their verse counts. The background dims so you can focus on the list, and one click outside the panel closes it.

The left panel also has buttons numbered 1 to 114 and arrow buttons for the previous or next surah. The surah you are reading is highlighted.

Search

05Search by surah name and verse number

Type a surah name followed by a verse number in the search box, for example At-Taubah: 16. The app opens that surah and highlights the verse.

It suits looking up a verse mentioned in a talk or an article.

Quick search

06Just the surah and verse numbers

Speakers usually mention only the surah number and verse number. Type 56.3 and the app opens surah 56 with verse 3 highlighted.

You do not need to remember surah names, so finding a verse is much faster.

Word search

07Find a word and see how many verses contain it

Type a single word, for example dunia, and the app lists every verse that contains it along with the total. In this example there are 205 verses.

The searched word is marked in yellow, and each result shows the surah name and verse number so it is easy to trace.

Reading mode 1

08Verse by verse

The most complete mode: each verse appears on its own with the Arabic text, the Latin reading, and then the translation.

It is comfortable for learning to read and understanding verses one at a time, with no scrolling to another place.

Reading mode 2

09Ruku block

The Arabic text flows as one block like in a mushaf, and the translation and reading for each verse are grouped below it.

It suits uninterrupted Arabic reading while still letting you check the meaning once the block ends.

Reading mode 3

10Two columns

The translation sits on the left and the Arabic text on the right, aligned verse by verse. Your eyes only move sideways to compare the Arabic and its meaning.

The width of the translation column can be adjusted in the Settings menu.

Presets

11Buttons C1 to C3 for your favorite layouts

Set the display the way you like, then save it to one of three preset buttons: C1, C2, or C3. One click and every setting returns to what you saved.

For example, make one preset that shows only the Arabic text without translation like a regular Qur'an, and another for full study with translation.

Highlight

12Click a verse to highlight it

Click a verse and it is highlighted in blue right away. Useful when teaching, discussing, or showing a particular verse to the person next to you.

The highlight also helps your eyes find the reading position again on a busy page.

Bookmarks

13Save your favorite verse positions

Press the save-position button to mark the surah and verse you are reading. The bookmark list shows the surah name and position, for example Ayat 22 dari 286.

Click a bookmark to jump straight there, or remove one with the X button. The count appears as a badge on the Bookmark button.

Zoom

14From small to very large

Adjust the display size with the Zoom slider. At 54% one screen holds many verses, while 130% or more suits anyone who finds small text hard to read.

Non-Arabic text size and translation column width have their own sliders too, so the display can be tuned to fit your eyes.

Window

15Maximize with a double click

Double-click the app frame to go full screen, and double-click again to return to the previous size. No need to hunt for a maximize button.

The frame stays neat at any window size.

Verse numbers

16Verse numbers and Arabic font your way

Verse numbers can use Arabic-style numerals like a mushaf or plain digits (1, 2, 3). There are five verse marker backgrounds: Ganda, Bunga, Bintang, Mandala, and Tiga, named as shown in the app.

You can also pick the Arabic font, Misbah or DigitalUmmah, and set text alignment (left, right, center, or justify) in the Settings menu.

Skins and frames

1730 color skins, 31 frame motifs, mix freely

Choose one of 30 color skins, then pair it with one of 31 geometric Islamic frame motifs. That makes more than 900 display combinations.

Skins change the color of the panel and buttons, from dark ones like Arang, Kopi, and Lava to light ones like Awan. Skin and frame names are shown as they appear in the app. The frame surrounds the whole window. Your choice is saved automatically.

And much more

Drag like a touch screen

Scroll and drag the screen up and down with the mouse, just like swiping a touch screen with a finger.

Resume where you left off

Your page and reading position are saved automatically. When you reopen the app, the last verse you read opens right away.

New version alert

When a newer version exists, the APP INFO button shows a NEW UPDATE badge, with the version history.

Auto scroll

The text moves up on its own so you can read or review without touching the mouse.

Latin reading

Show the Latin reading with long vowel markers (aa, ii, uu) to help with pronunciation.

Desktop shortcut

One button in Settings creates an app shortcut on your Desktop.

Free

18Free for everyone

Al-Qur'an Digital is free for the public and has no ads. If you find it useful, support through Saweria goes a long way toward its continued development.
