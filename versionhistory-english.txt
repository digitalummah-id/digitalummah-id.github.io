Version 1.00
============
Al-Qur'an Digital by DigitalUmmah, first release.

- Portable app: one EXE, one Qur'an data file, one language file, no installation.
- Arabic text of 114 surahs and 6,236 verses follows the Kemenag Qur'an.
- Punctuation, waqf marks, and verse numbers match Kemenag.
- Two embedded Arabic fonts: DigitalUmmah and LPMQ Isep Misbah.
- Fonts load from inside the app data, no need to install them in Windows.
- Font changes apply instantly and your choice is remembered.
- Verse numbers can use Arabic style or plain digits.
- Show Surah panel to open surahs 1 to 114.
- Arrow buttons to jump to the previous or next surah.
- Search by surah name, verse number, and word.
- Translation under every verse, can be turned on or off.
- Latin transliteration to help with pronunciation.
- Long vowel markers (aa, ii, uu) in Latin text.
- Footnotes shown separately and can be hidden.
- Full reading mode up to the ruku for a mushaf-like view.
- Verse bookmarks with a count badge and direct jump to the saved position.
- Three quick slots: C1, C2, and C3.
- Auto scroll with adjustable speed.
- Display zoom.
- Color skins and geometric Islamic frame motifs.
- Settings are saved automatically.
- Interface languages: Indonesian and English.
- Free and ad-free.
