Full Features & User Guide

Each feature explained, with how to use it. Click the image box beside each section to see the full-size screenshot.

Portable

01One app, no installation

Al-Qur'an Digital is built to stay light. The app is just one EXE file, one Qur'an data file (alquran.qre), and one language file. There is no installer and no complicated setup. Extract the ZIP and run it.

Both Arabic fonts are stored inside the data file and loaded straight into memory when the app starts. You do not need to install any font into Windows, and the Arabic text looks the same on every computer.

Accuracy

02Arabic text that follows Qur'an Kemenag

All 114 surahs and 6,236 verses follow the Qur'an Kemenag text, including punctuation, pause marks, and verse numbers. Nothing is shortened or paraphrased.

Each verse ends with an ornamental number circle, so the boundary between verses stays easy to spot in long passages.

Typography

03Two Arabic font choices

Choose between Hanzo Naskh and LPMQ Isep Misbah in Settings. Hanzo Naskh is a modern naskh style with neat diacritics, while LPMQ Isep Misbah is the Indonesian Standard Mushaf font that many readers already know.

The font changes instantly without restarting, and your choice is remembered for next time.

Navigation

04All 114 surahs in one panel

The left panel shows buttons numbered 1 to 114. Click a number and the surah opens from the very first line. The left and right arrows take you to the previous or next surah.

The surah currently open is highlighted in blue, so you always know where you are.

Search

05Search by surah, verse, or word

The search box in the left panel accepts a surah name, a verse number, or a word. Results appear as a list, and one click takes you straight to that verse.

When there are too many matches, the app shows a limited first set so the list stays fast and easy to scan.

Translation

06Translation under every verse

The translation appears right below its verse. There is no need to switch pages or open another window to understand the meaning.

You can turn the translation on or off, so you can focus on the Arabic text alone when reviewing your memorization.

Reading aid

07Latin reading to help with pronunciation

For readers who are not yet fluent in Arabic script, each verse comes with a Latin transliteration. It follows the Kemenag data, so it stays consistent with the Arabic above it.

The Latin line can be hidden at any time to keep the page clean.

Notes

08Footnotes that stay tidy

Some verses carry footnotes that explain terms or context. The app shows them as a separate section so the verse text is never interrupted.

Like other features, footnotes can be shown or hidden from Settings.

Bookmarks

09Bookmarks to resume reading

Mark the verse you are reading and come back to it any time with the Bookmark button. A blue badge on the button shows how many bookmarks you have saved.

When you open a verse from the bookmark list, the app takes you to the marked position instead of the start of the surah.

Quick slots

10Three quick slots: C1, C2, and C3

The C1, C2, and C3 buttons in the left panel are shortcuts for readings you open often, such as a surah you recite every morning or evening.

When you click a slot, the view jumps straight to the top of the reading.

Tajwid

11Long vowel markers

The Long vowels (aa, ii, uu) option helps you recognize elongated sounds in the Latin text. Turn it on when you want to practice, and off when you prefer a simpler look.

Comfort

12Auto scroll for hands-free reading

Turn on Auto scroll and the text moves up on its own. It suits long reading sessions or reviewing while you hold a mushaf or other notes.

The speed can be adjusted, and you can stop it at any time.

Settings

13A complete Settings menu

Settings gathers the Arabic font, Latin display, translation, footnotes, long vowels, auto scroll, and bold non-Arabic text. Every change shows on screen immediately.

Settings are saved automatically, so the app opens exactly as you left it.

Language

14Indonesian and English interface

All button and menu text follows the selected language file. Indonesian and English packs are available, and either can be swapped without touching the Qur'an data.

Because language lives in its own file, more languages can be added later without rebuilding the app.

Look and feel

15Dark interface with an ornamental frame

The navigation panel is dark while the reading area is bright with strong contrast. Together they keep your eyes comfortable during long sessions.

A geometric Islamic ornament frames the window, with no floral or figurative motifs, which keeps it fitting for the Qur'an.

Free

16Free for everyone

Al-Qur'an Digital is free for the public and has no ads. If you find it useful, support through Saweria goes a long way toward its continued development.
